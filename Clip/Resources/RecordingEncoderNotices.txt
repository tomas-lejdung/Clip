Clip recording encoder

Clip invokes a standalone FFmpeg executable to process recordings. This build
contains FFmpeg 8.0 and x264 r3222 (revision b35605a), licensed under GNU GPL
version 2 or later. Neither component is linked into the Clip application.

Contents/Resources/RecordingEncoderSources. No source modifications are made.
The encoder uses macOS system frameworks and the statically linked x264 above;
it has no Homebrew or other separately installed runtime dependencies.
