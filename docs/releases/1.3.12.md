# Clip 1.3.12

Clip 1.3.12 refreshes the menu-bar experience and gives fullscreen Live Share
hosts control over which apps contribute audio.

## Popovers and controls

- Use one consistent card, row, toggle, navigation, and button system across
  recording, Live Share, stream settings, and native viewer controls.
- Keep popover height fluid as content changes, with compact spacing, aligned
  controls, full-row hover feedback, and clear primary actions.
- Simplify room preparation, source selection, viewer controls, and streaming
  settings without changing their behavior.

## Live Share audio

- Exclude selected applications from fullscreen system-audio capture, helping
  prevent callers from hearing their own Discord or conferencing audio.
- Apply audio exclusions live and preserve the existing app-window audio
  behavior.

## Requirements

Clip requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. This build uses the same
stable Apple Development identity as previous updater releases.
