# Clip 1.5.1

Fixes Copy and drag exports in the optimized app build, and makes trimming an explicit step.

- Choose **Trim** to reveal the range handles, then **Save Trim** to prepare the shareable MP4. Copy and drag become available when preparation completes.
- Outside trim editing, the timeline shows the saved range without handles, and playback stays within that range. Cancel restores the saved trim.
- Copy and drag reuse a prepared trim, including a drag completed after Preview closes. The complete Source stays intact for later trim changes.
- Preserves the native-resolution HEVC capture and CRF 24 H.264 processing introduced in the 1.5.0 review build.

No Live Share protocol or server changes.
