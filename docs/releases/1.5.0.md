# Clip 1.5.0

Draft: recording changes only. Add the other PR’s changes after both PRs are merged, then prepare the release.

- Capture full-resolution screen detail, including macOS HiDPI pixels on external displays, into a temporary HEVC recording. Preview automatically processes it into a shareable H.264 MP4.
- Choose Native, 75%, 50%, or 25% resolution and a CRF from 18 to 40 in the first Preview. Native resolution and CRF 24 are the defaults; successful choices are remembered for the next recording.
- Adjust recording bitrate in Settings → Recording. The default is 10 Mbps, and changes apply to new recordings.
- Keep the complete processed Source when Preview closes and discard the temporary original. Reopen the Source later to revise trimming without losing the untrimmed video.
- Use **Trim**, then **Save Trim**, to prepare a shareable edit. Copy and drag reuse that prepared file. The timeline shows the saved range with rounded ends and reveals its handles only while editing.
- Open an enlarged native player for a better preview. Clicking the menu-bar icon during recording now stops capture and opens Preview directly.
- Fix Copy and drag failures, repeated-trim errors, error dialogs that did not respond to clicks, and duplicate `.mp4` filename extensions.
- Replace the old Crisp, Compact, and Smallest recording presets with the new resolution and CRF controls, and remove the retired encoding paths and prototype code.

Existing recordings and preferences remain readable.
